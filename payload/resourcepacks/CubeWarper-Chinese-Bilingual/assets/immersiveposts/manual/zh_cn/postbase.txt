可延长立柱（Extendable Post）

向天空延伸。（The Sky's the Limit.）

<&postbase>用作自定义尺寸立柱的底座。

（使用方法请参阅<link;immersiveposts:usage;§1使用说明§r;start>。）

手持方块右键点击即可伪装底座，使其显示为该方块的外观。<np>

潜行时右键点击可移除伪装。

仅可使用实体且不透明的方块作为伪装。<np>

English original

Used as the start of the custom-sized posts.
(See <link;immersiveposts:usage;§1Usage§r;start> on how to use it.)
The base can be hidden simply by holding a block in your hand, right-clicking, and it will pretend to be that block.<np>
Sneak + right-click removes the cover.
It only accepts solid and opaque blocks as cover, however.
