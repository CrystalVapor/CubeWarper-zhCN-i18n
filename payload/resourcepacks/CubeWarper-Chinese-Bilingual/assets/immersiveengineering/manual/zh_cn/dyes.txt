工业染料（Industrial Dyes）

绝对完全无害（Definitely Perfectly Harmless）

金属化合物在工业染料中有广泛用途，其中最实用的是铅。

铅化合物可以制成大量红色和白色染料。

由于制造铬酸铅所需的部分原料难以大量获取，无法使用铅大量生产黄色染料。

<&lead_white>将腐烂物质产生的二氧化碳与变质乙醇中的乙酸结合，可以将金属铅制成§l碳酸铅§r，通常称为铅白。

这种制法无需大量刷取骷髅并研磨其骨头来获取染料。

<&lead_red>在§2熔炉§r中高温氧化铅粒，可以将金属铅转化为§l氧化铅§r，通常称为铅丹。

这种染料的生产无需使用花卉农场或园艺玻璃罩种植大量红色花朵。<np>

English original

Metal compounds have many uses in industrial dyes, and the most useful of these is lead.
Lead compounds can be crafted into large quantities of red and white dye.
Yellow dye has not been produced in quantity from lead, as several ingredients cannot be found in quantity to make lead chromate.
By combining the carbon dioxide fumes from rotting material and the acetic acid from spoiled ethanol, lead metal can be crafted into §lLead Carbonate§r, commonly known as Lead White.
Dye produced this way can be made without the need to farm copious numbers of skeletons to grind their bones.
By oxidizing lead metal nuggets in a §2Furnace§r at high temperature, lead metal can be converted into §lLead Oxide§r, more commonly known as Red Lead.
This dye can be produced without the need for a flower farm or the cloche for copious amounts of red flowers.
